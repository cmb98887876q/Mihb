01

Copyright / DMCA Requests

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that content or a link on this website infringes your copyright, you may submit a formal DMCA notice. We take valid copyright complaints seriously and will review them promptly.

02

Information Required

To process a DMCA request, please include the following:

  • Full name and company/organization name where applicable
  • Valid contact email address
  • Exact URL(s) on this website being reported
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization to act on behalf of the owner
  • A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
03

Game Rules / Terms of Service Violations

If you believe that a link published on our website promotes, references, or provides access to tools that violate a game’s official rules or Terms of Service, you may submit a report for review.

Examples of such tools may include:

  • Cheats
  • Unauthorized game modifications
  • Aimbots
  • Wallhacks
  • Other unauthorized gameplay tools
04

Information Required for Game Rule Reports

When submitting a report related to game rules or Terms of Service, please provide:

  • Your name and contact email
  • The exact URL on this website where the link or content appears
  • A clear description of why the linked material may violate the game’s rules
  • Any supporting information that helps us review the report
05

Review & Removal Process

After receiving a valid report, we will review the reported page or link. Where appropriate, we may remove or disable access to the content or link under our control.

Incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, or abusive reports may require additional clarification before any action is taken.

06

Third-Party Content & External Links

This website does not control third-party servers and cannot directly remove files or content hosted on external websites. We can only remove references or links that appear on our own pages.

If the content is hosted elsewhere, you may also need to contact the relevant third-party website operator or hosting provider to request removal.

07

Good-Faith Reporting

All reports should be submitted in good faith. Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and supported by relevant details. False or abusive reports may be disregarded.
